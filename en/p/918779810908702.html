Happy Lohri wishing card given to me by some sweet kids

13 January 2026

This poem is in Hindi, as Meena Kumari wrote it.

Share on WhatsApp

The poem as text

Happy Lohri
aunty

You are the Best aunty in the world
From Jaspreet
God will give all bless of all to you
From Alina
You are no aunty for us you are friend of us
From manpreet
You are the most beautiful aunty in the world
From Harline
God will give you all thing to
because you are queen of all the kids
From Kiran
we love you aunty
we will thank to
because you will
give all achivements
to us
From Aunty
Thank you aunty for everything

सुंदर मुंदरिए होए
तेरा कौन बिचारा, होए
दुल्ला भट्टीवाला, होए
दुल्ले दी धी ब्याही, होए
सेर शक्कर पाई, होए
कुड़ी दा लाल पटाका, होए ।
कुड़ी दा सालू पाटा, होए ।।
सालू कौन समेटे, होए ।

Happy Lohri wishing card given to me by some sweet kids.❤️💕
God bless them all always. 🙏🏼
